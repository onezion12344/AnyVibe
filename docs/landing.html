How It Works

📞
Step 1
You Call the Yellow Sheep
Put on your earphones. Tap call. Speak your task in natural language — like calling a colleague. The CS receptionist (黄羊) listens, powered by StepFun realtime voice AI.
🧠
Step 2
The Brain Decides & Dispatches
A fast text LLM reads your transcript, classifies intent, and dispatches the work to a Qoder CEO — a persistent AI boss that assembles the right role team for the job.
👥
Step 3
The Role Team Gets to Work
Architect plans. Builder codes. Reviewer checks. Tester validates. Every agent's progress streams onto a live kanban board — you can watch the company work in real time.
📲
Step 4
The Agent Calls YOU Back
When the work is done, the CEO calls you back — directly to your phone. Accept the call, hear the summary. No screen-checking. No polling. The differentiator Codex doesn't have.

Architecture

See It In Action

Kanban Board — 4-column company view: Backlog, Running, Needs-Approval, Done. Every agent's progress is LLM-summarized and streamed live.
Architecture Overview — Full system: voice in, CS receptionist, Qoder CEO fan-out to role team, kanban, and callback to user.
Always-On Companion — Cycling, walking, driving. No keyboard. No screen. Just your earphones and your voice. Code anywhere.
Qoder Backend — Persistent CEO agent manages a preset role team (Architect, Builder, Reviewer, Tester). Fan-out via Qoder Agent SDK.
Viaim SDK Integration — 科大讯飞 headset SDK provides the always-on earphone voice input pipeline.

Core Features

Voice-First Interface

Call in from your earphones. Speak naturally in English or Chinese. The Yellow Sheep receptionist listens and understands.

Bidirectional Calling

AI Role Team
